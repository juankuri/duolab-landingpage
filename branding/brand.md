# Brand

## Identity

DuoLab is a clinical analysis and pathology laboratory in Ciudad del Carmen, Campeche.

The brand should feel close, clear, professional, and reassuring. It should not feel like a cold institution or a generic medical template.

## Audience

Primary audience:

Carla, 34, mother of young children in Ciudad del Carmen. She wants a trustworthy laboratory, clear prices, simple preparation instructions, and patient treatment for children who may be afraid of needles.

Secondary audience:

Roberto, 42, adult patient who wants to complete requested studies quickly. He cares about price clarity, waiting time, preparation requirements, and reliable results.

## Personality

Cercano
: Warm and human, not institutional.

Claro
: Plain language. If a patient would not understand a term, rewrite it.

Profesional
: Expertise without arrogance.

Tranquilizador
: Patients often arrive with anxiety. The brand should meet that with calm.

## Voice Rules

- Say "Más de 50 análisis disponibles. Sin cita. Sin sorpresas de precio."
- Do not say "Realizamos estudios de diagnóstico clínico de alta complejidad."
- Say "Si tu hijo le tiene miedo a las agujas, aquí sabemos cómo tratarlo."
- Do not use technical or specialty language when plain language works better.
- Do not use "Contáctenos".

## Logo

The logo combines typographic elements with a laboratory tube icon. The arc represents movement, precision, and relationship between the two parts.

Rules:

- Do not change the logo colors outside the approved palette.
- Do not distort or change proportions.
- Do not use it on backgrounds with insufficient contrast.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, or effects not defined by the brand.
- Do not separate the icon from the text in formal communications.
- Keep clear space around the logo equivalent to at least the height of the "d".

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is the primary typeface for digital communication.

Use weight to establish hierarchy:

- ExtraBold 800 for main headlines and primary CTAs.
- Bold 700 for section headings.
- SemiBold 600 for card titles and labels.
- Medium 500 for body emphasis.
- Regular 400 for body copy.

## Anti-References

- Cold institutional healthcare language.
- Medical jargon where plain language works.
- Generic SaaS landing patterns.
- Placeholder testimonials.
- Stock-photo sterility when concrete trust signals are available.
